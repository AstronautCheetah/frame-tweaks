# Hello pack

An example pack with one tweak, to copy when you write your own: see
[docs/mods.md](../../docs/mods.md). To try it, copy this folder into
`~/.local/share/frame-tweaks/packs/`, then trust it (installer window → 🧩 Packs, or
`./install.sh mods trust "Hello pack"`). Its `.github/workflows/pack-check.yml` checks a pack on
each change once the pack has a repository of its own (docs/mods.md, Sharing a pack).
