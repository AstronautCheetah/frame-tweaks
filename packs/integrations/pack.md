makers' own releases or Flathub where Frame Tweaks installs them, and set up with the parts that
make them work well on the Frame, such as a tray icon, a key kept in the wallet, or menu entries.

Frame Tweaks' other official packs, with the same categories; it's on unless you turn it off on
the Packs page.
