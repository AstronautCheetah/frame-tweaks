Other projects' apps and services, fitted to the Steam Frame by Frame Tweaks: installed from their
makers' own releases, Flathub, or Frame Tweaks' own build of them (Shared clipboard), and set up
with the parts that make them work well on the Frame, such as a tray icon, a key kept in the
wallet, or menu entries.

How it's loaded: like [Original](../original/pack.md) and [3rd Party](../third-party/pack.md),
Frame Tweaks' other official packs, with the same categories; it's on unless you turn it off on
the Packs page.
