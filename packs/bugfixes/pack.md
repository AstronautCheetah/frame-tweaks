Frame Tweaks' fixes for problems in SteamOS, SteamVR and Steam themselves, until Valve fixes
them: each one works around a bug found on a Frame, and checks whether your Frame still has it.
A fix your SteamOS no longer needs isn't offered.

How it's loaded: like [Core](../core/pack.md), [Integrations](../integrations/pack.md) and
[3rd Party](../third-party/pack.md), Frame Tweaks' other official packs, with the same
categories; it's on unless you turn it off on the Packs page.
