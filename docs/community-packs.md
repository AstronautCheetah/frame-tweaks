# Community packs

Packs of tweaks that other people share. To try one: in the Frame Tweaks window, **🧩 Packs →
Add a pack from a link** with its link (or `./install.sh mods add LINK`). Then read what its
tweaks do, and trust it if you do: until then, nothing of it runs.

**Listed isn't endorsed.** Before a pack is listed, a maintainer checks that it loads (`mods
check`) and reads it for anything harmful, at the commit named here. Later changes aren't
checked: **Update** brings them, and the pack then waits until you trust it again, so read
what changed. A pack's tweaks run as you, with your files and passwords in reach.

## Getting yours listed

Put the pack in a git repository of its own, add the pack check to it, then open a
[Share a pack](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/issues/new?template=share_a_pack.yml)
issue with its link and the commit to check: [Sharing a pack](mods.md#sharing-a-pack). A pack
whose tweaks would suit Frame Tweaks itself can also go into it:
[Sending a tweak to Frame Tweaks](mods.md#sending-a-tweak-to-frame-tweaks).
