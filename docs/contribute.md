# Help make Frame Tweaks better

Frame Tweaks is made by Steam Frame owners for Steam Frame owners, and there's room for more
hands. You don't need to write code for most of these, and none of it needs much time.

## Ideas

- **Suggest a third-party app or project to add.** Something you already use on your Frame that
  others would want: a tool, a VR utility, a better keyboard. Say where it lives and why it
  helps; [a tweak idea](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/issues/new?template=tweak_idea.yml).
- **Describe a tweak nobody has written yet.** Something the Frame should do and doesn't: a
  missing button, a fix you keep making by hand, a setting that never sticks. Rough ideas are
  welcome; [a tweak idea](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/issues/new?template=tweak_idea.yml).

## Testing and reports

- **Report a bug.** Use **🐞 Report a problem** at the top of this window: it saves a
  diagnostics report (names and addresses taken out) and opens the bug form with your version
  filled in.
- **Say what works on your Frame.** Which tweaks work, or don't, on your SteamOS and SteamVR
  versions helps everyone after an update;
  [works on my Frame](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/issues/new?template=test_report.yml).
- **Try the beta channel** (`FRAME_TWEAKS_CHANNEL=beta`, or `beta` in
  `~/.config/frame-tweaks/channel`) and say how a release candidate does before it's out.

## Translations

- **Review a translation** in a language you speak: wording that reads oddly, a term that isn't
  what SteamVR or KDE call it, a text that doesn't fit its button. Translations marked as needing
  review are listed in the [Translations needed](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Atranslations)
  issue.
- **Add a language.** The texts go out as files you can translate in any editor or tool, and
  come back checked: [translating Frame Tweaks](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/blob/main/locale/README.md).

## Code and docs

- **Fix or improve a tweak, or write a new one,** in English only if you like: the maintainers
  translate before each release. [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  has the layout of a tweak and the checks to run.
- **Make your own pack** of tweaks, for yourself or to share:
  [packs of tweaks](https://github.com/AstronautCheetah/frame-tweaks/blob/main/docs/mods.md).
- **Improve the docs:** a step that confused you, a missing screenshot, a "Common issues" entry.

## Spreading the word

- **Tell other Frame owners** about the tweaks that help you, and star the project on GitHub so
  others find it.
